Healthcare AI Solutions

We build AI systems for clinical data analysis, risk prediction, and decision support in U.S. healthcare settings. Most of our healthcare work comes down to turning unstructured clinical information — faxes, PDFs, scanned records, and EHR/EMR exports — into structured data that clinical and operations teams can act on with confidence.

Key Capabilities & Architecture

  • Agentic RAG Pipelines: Clinical and financial data pipelines that turn unstructured records into structured insight using agentic RAG.
  • HIPAA-Aligned Architecture: HIPAA-aligned architecture for handling protected health information through extraction, analysis, and storage.
  • Predictive Risk Analytics: Predictive analytics to support clinical resource planning and early risk identification.
  • Traceable Extraction: Document extraction built for healthcare — medical faxes, scanned forms, and clinical PDFs — with field-level traceability.
