GOVERNANCE & TRANS-PROVIDER

Operational Due Diligence & Audits.

Request DDQ
  • Who are the firm's prime brokers?
    Our primary brokerage and custodial accounts are maintained at Tier 1 global investment institutions (such as Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley) to ensure trading settlement security.
  • Who administers the systematic investment programs?
    To ensure absolute bookkeeping and valuation transparency, our funds are managed under the administration of independent third-party fund administrators (such as SS&C and Citco).
  • How are the assets and systematic valuations audited?
    We undergo complete annual audits performed by Tier 1 accounting networks (such as PwC and EY) to certify all asset holdings, valuations, and compliance metrics.
  • What regulatory frameworks govern the funds?
    Our corporate structures strictly operate under relevant regulatory exemptions (such as US SEC Reg D 506(c) exemptions for verified qualified purchasers or respective local AIF frameworks).
  • How is security posture and data safety enforced?
    Our environments employ mandatory MFA, collocated servers, zero public registration gateways, regular third-party pen tests, and are structured around NIST SP 800-63B guidelines to ensure allocator confidentiality.
