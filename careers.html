Culture & Philosophy

We are first and foremost a math and technology company. We are tinkerers, mathematicians, computer scientists, and engineers working as one team to solve incredibly difficult problems, and trading millions of shares a day on the world's financial markets.

We go all in on talent, rewarding creativity in a fast-paced environment where capable, motivated people thrive.

01.
Process Transparency

Clear evaluation metrics, open research sharing, and objective meritocracy where the best ideas win regardless of seniority.

02.
Intellectual Challenge

Tackle frontier problems in high-dimensional statistical learning, stochastic calculus, and sub-microsecond systems.

03.
Collaborative Culture

Ego-free environment where world-class researchers and engineers collaborate seamlessly across cross-functional teams.
