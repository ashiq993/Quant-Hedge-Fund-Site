WHO WE ARE

Engineering AI that works under real operating conditions.

Adople AI is a deep-tech startup that builds generative AI, autonomous agents, and RAG-enabled knowledge bases for regulated industries like healthcare and finance. We create multi-agent systems and LLM-enabled workflows that can work within the framework of HIPAA, SOC 2, and corporate data governance policies, making our solutions suitable for the regulated world from the start rather than in a lab setting.

Our Approaches

We develop practical AI solutions utilizing generative AI and multi-agent architectures to address real-world business challenges in the form of applications, scalable LLM platforms, and autonomous systems for healthcare, financial, and enterprise settings.

Our objective is to become the definitive benchmark for enterprise AI, allowing companies to implement multi-agent and LLM-based solutions in a precise and efficient manner.

GLOBAL REACH

Global Presence

The engineering arm of Adople AI is based in Salem, Tamil Nadu, India, where the core development and research groups operate from. The office for working with our clients is based in San Francisco, California, serving healthcare and financial institutions in the USA. Thus, we have access to a well-honed engineering team alongside a group that understands the American regulations.
