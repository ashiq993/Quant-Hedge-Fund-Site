Interview Transparency

We value your time. Here is the strict, objective process by which we assess and onboard technical and research talent.

  • Phase 1: Technical Screen
    A 45-minute technical phone screen focused on fundamental algorithms, data structures, complexity analysis, and modern system components.
  • Phase 2: The Take-Home Challenge
    A timed, asynchronous coding challenge (focusing on data pipeline construction or quantitative math puzzles) to evaluate your practical software engineering and problem-solving capability.
  • Phase 3: On-Site Design & Presentation
    An interactive series of deep whiteboarding sessions, system design architecture discussions, and a quantitative research presentation with our core team.
